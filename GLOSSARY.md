# Cadence

Cadence is an online course platform where learners buy video courses from instructors and watch them at their own pace.

## Language

### Purchasing

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to give back a course they bought and get their money returned, with their reason for asking. An Admin reviews it by hand, judging it on the facts as they stood when the learner asked. It is open to the learner only within a short window after buying and before they have watched much of the course — counting what they watched under any earlier purchase of it.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Pending**:
The state of a Refund Request that is waiting for someone to review it. A Pending Refund Request ends either Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request that has been accepted, which results in a Refund. The learner loses access to the course the moment it is Approved, but their progress in it is kept, so they can pick up where they left off if they buy it again.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request that has been turned down; no money is returned and the learner keeps the course. The Admin always tells the learner why.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner's card when a Refund Request is Approved. A Refund is always exactly what the learner paid for the course, never part of it and never more, even if the course price has changed since.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, partial refund
