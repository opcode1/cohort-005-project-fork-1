# Cadence

Cadence is an online course platform where learners buy video courses from instructors and watch them at their own pace.

## Language

### Purchasing

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to give back a course they bought and get their money returned, with their reason for asking. Someone reviews it by hand, and it is open to the learner only within a short window after buying and before they have watched much of the course.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Pending**:
The state of a Refund Request that is waiting for someone to review it. A Pending Refund Request ends either Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request that has been accepted, which results in a Refund.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request that has been turned down; no money is returned.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner's card when a Refund Request is Approved. A Refund is always the full amount the course is refundable for, never part of it.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, partial refund
